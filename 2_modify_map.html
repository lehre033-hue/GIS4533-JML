Jared's Favorite Places in Duluth, MN

by: Jared Lehrer

This map shows some of my favorite places in Duluth, MN. I have added markers for the University of Minnesota Duluth campus, the Duluth Shipping Pier, Gary New Duluth Skatepark, the Hawk Ridge Nature Reserve, the Lake Superior Railroad Museum, and Enger Tower. Each marker has a popup with a photo and a fun fact about the location.
