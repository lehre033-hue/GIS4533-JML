Duluth Points of Interest for FDI World Dental Congress Attendees

by: Jared Lehrer

Welcome to Duluth, Minnesota. This interactive map is designed to help attendees explore popular tourist attractions throughout the city. This resource is intended to help visitors make the most of their time in Duluth by providing a spatial representation of the experiences this city has to offer. Click the blue markers on the map below to learn more about each point of interest. Each marker contains a location description, a photo, and a link to the attraction's website for more information. Enjoy your visit to Duluth!
