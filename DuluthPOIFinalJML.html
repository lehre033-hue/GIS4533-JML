Duluth Points of Interest for FDI World Dental Congress Attendees

by: Jared Lehrer

Welcome to Duluth, Minnesota. This interactive map is designed to assist attendees of the FDI World Dental Congress 2026 in exploring notable attractions throughout the city. Each marker provides information, images, and details about popular local destinations. This resource is intended to help visitors make the most of their time in Duluth by providing a spatial representation of the experiences this city has to offer.
